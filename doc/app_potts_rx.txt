app_style potts/rx command :h3
app_style potts/rx/kk command :h3

[Syntax:]

app_style style Q keyword values ... :pre

style = {potts/rx} or {potts/rx/kk} :ulb,l
Q = number of spin states of the deformed grains :l
zero or more keyword/value pairs may be appended :l
keyword = {nucleation} or {hardening} :l
  {nucleation} values = rate Hmin {boundary}
    rate = nucleation probability per site per unit time (MCS)
    Hmin = only sites with stored energy >= Hmin nucleate
    {boundary} = optional word: only sites on a grain boundary nucleate
  {hardening} values = rate recovery
    rate, recovery = every site's stored energy grows as
                     dH/dt = rate - recovery*H :pre
:ule

[Examples:]

app_style potts/rx 1000
app_style potts/rx 1000 nucleation 0.0002 0.5
app_style potts/rx/kk 1000 nucleation 0.0002 0.5
app_style potts/rx 1000 hardening 0.1 0.02 nucleation 0.0005 3.0 boundary :pre

[Description:]

This is an on-lattice application which evolves a Q-state Potts model
of static or dynamic recrystallization. Each site has a spin (i1) and a stored
energy H (d1). Spins 1 to Q are deformed grains; spins above Q are
recrystallized grains. The energy of site I is

Ei = Sum_j delta_ij + Hi :pre

where Sum_j is a sum over the neighbors of site I, delta_ij is 0 if the
spins of sites I and J are the same and 1 if they differ, and the
boundary energy J = 1 sets the unit of H and of the
"temperature"_temperature.html.

A rKMC event at site I picks a random neighbor J. If J has another spin,
site I would take J's spin and stored energy. The change in energy dE
is the change in the number of unlike neighbors of I plus HJ - HI, and
the flip is accepted with probability min\[1,exp(-dE/kT)\]. Boundary
energy and stored energy enter the same test, so a recrystallization
front moves by the stored-energy difference across it and by its
curvature, and grain growth runs everywhere, in the deformed grains,
the recrystallized grains and across the front, under one
Hamiltonian.

Nuclei are sites with spin > Q and H = 0. Set them before a run
(site-saturated nucleation) with the "set"_set.html command, or let
the app make them with the {nucleation} keyword (continuous
nucleation): after every sweep, each deformed site with H >= Hmin
seeds a nucleus with probability rate*dt, and the site and its
deformed neighbors become recrystallized grain Q + its site ID, with
H = 0. A one-site nucleus shrinks at once, which is why the seed takes
its neighbors. With the optional word {boundary}, only sites with a
neighbor of another spin seed. The draws are keyed to the site ID and
the sweep, so the nuclei do not depend on the number of processors.

The {hardening} keyword makes the model one of dynamic
recrystallization: after every sweep the stored energy of every site,
recrystallized or not, grows as dH/dt = rate - recovery*H (integrated
exactly over the sweep), which saturates at rate/recovery, or grows
without bound if recovery = 0. With hardening, recrystallized grains
can nucleate again and their sites can join a new nucleus; a nucleus
seeded in a recrystallized grain takes a hashed spin above Q + the
number of sites (that two such grains get the same spin is unlikely,
and matters only if they touch). Without hardening the model is one of
static recrystallization: only deformed sites (spin <= Q) seed or
join a nucleus, and the stored energy changes only by the flips and
nucleation.

A nucleus or front grows when the stored energy it removes outweighs
the boundary it adds. On sc/26n at T = 0.5 a 3x3x3 nucleus shrinks at
H = 1, grows slowly at H = 2, and recrystallizes a deformed grain
structure in about 80 MCS at H = 4. When H >= maxneigh - 2 any
deformed site with one recrystallized neighbor can flip downhill,
whatever the shape of its boundary: the front then grows without
curvature, like an Eden cluster, and comes out rough. The app warns if
the stored energy of a deformed site, or the hardening saturation
value, reaches that bound; keep H well
below it ("Williamson and Delplanque"_#Williamson15 discuss the
stored-energy limit of Potts models).

Time is in Monte Carlo steps (one sweep = 1). The stats output adds a
column Xrx, the fraction of sites with spin > Q (ever recrystallized). The "diag_style
energy"_diag_energy.html diagnostic sums Ei over the sites.

i2 and d2 are the app's own: i2 holds the nucleus seeds of the last
sweep, d2 exp(-H/kT), which the app sets at the start of each run. Do
not set them.

This application runs only with rKMC, with any "sweep"_sweep.html
method; it does not have a KMC solver.

:line

Style {potts/rx/kk} is a Kokkos version of the same model, which runs
the sweep and the nucleation in parallel on a GPU or with OpenMP
threads. It takes the same arguments and sweeps with "sweep
color"_sweep.html or "sweep color/strict"_sweep.html; with
{color/strict} it gives the same answer as {potts/rx}, bit for bit, on
any number of processors.

:line

The following additional commands are defined by this application:

"temperature"_temperature.html : set Monte Carlo temperature :tb(s=:)

:line

[Restrictions:]

At most 32 neighbors per site. With {nucleation}, Q + the number of
sites must fit in an int.

Style {potts/rx/kk} is part of the KOKKOS package.  It is only enabled
if SPPARKS was built with that package.  See the "Making
SPPARKS"_Section_start.html#start_3 section for more info.

[Related commands:]

"app_style potts"_app_potts.html

[Default:] no nucleation, no hardening
