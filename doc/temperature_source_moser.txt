:line

temperature_source moser :h3

[Syntax:]

setup_temperature_source moser           Q lambda k alpha T0 cp sx sy sz
setup_temperature_source moser standard  Q lambda k alpha T0 cp sx sy sz
setup_temperature_source moser keyhole   Q lambda k alpha T0 cp &
    f_top sx_top sy_top sz_top z_top &
    f_bot sx_bot sy_bot sz_bot z_bot
setup_temperature_source moser card \[Q v\] \[lambda v\] \[T0 v\] \[spot sx sy sz\] :pre

mode = omitted or {standard} (single ellipsoid), or {keyhole}
       (two overlapping ellipsoids, surface cap + depth lobe) :ulb,l
Q = laser power (W) :l
lambda = absorptivity (-); absorbed power = lambda * Q :l
k = thermal conductivity (W/(m K)) :l
alpha = thermal diffusivity (m^2/s); with cp gives the density rho = k/(alpha cp) :l
T0 = ambient / preheat temperature (K) :l
cp = specific heat capacity (J/(kg K)) :l
sx, sy, sz = source widths (m) along the scan direction, transverse, and in depth;
             the source is exp(-x^2/sx^2 - y^2/sy^2 - z^2/sz^2), so each is a
             1/e radius (sqrt(2) times the Gaussian standard deviation) :l
f_top, f_bot = fractions of the absorbed power lambda*Q in each lobe;
               each >= 0, summing to 1 within 1e-6 (keyhole only) :l
sx_top, sy_top, sz_top = top-lobe widths (m) (keyhole only) :l
z_top = depth of the top-lobe center below the laser plane (m), >= 0 (keyhole only) :l
sx_bot, sy_bot, sz_bot = bottom-lobe widths (m) (keyhole only) :l
z_bot = depth of the bottom-lobe center below the laser plane (m), >= 0 (keyhole only) :l
card = take the {standard} arguments from the loaded "material"_material.html
       card; see "setup_temperature_source"_setup_temperature_source.html :l,ule

[Examples:]

setup_temperature_source moser 340.0 0.355 11.2 2.0959e-6 573.0 650.0 50e-6 50e-6 75e-6
setup_temperature_source moser 176 0.9 22.5 4.335e-6 473 600 20e-6 20e-6 10e-6 :pre

setup_temperature_source moser keyhole 340.0 0.155 11.2 2.0959e-6 573.0 650.0 &
    0.55  60e-6 60e-6  40e-6  0.0 &
    0.45  35e-6 35e-6 100e-6  120e-6 :pre

See examples/am/surge/validation (in.moser_pause.cpu, in.moser_pause_below.kk,
...) and examples/am/surge/gp700_moser/in.gp700_thermal.

[Description:]

Implements an unsteady time-domain Green's-function solution for the
temperature around an ellipsoidal Gaussian heat source moving along a
path over a half-space.  Selected via
"setup_temperature_source"_setup_temperature_source.html with type
{moser}.  All quantities are SI.  Two modes are available.

{standard} (single ellipsoid).  The temperature at site (x,y,z) and
time t is

T(x,y,z,t) - T0 = (1 / (rho cp)) * integral_0^t G(x - xs(t'),
                  y - ys(t'), z - zL, t - t') q(t') dt' :pre

where (xs, ys, zL) is the laser path, q(t') the absorbed power, and G
the Gaussian-source Green's function with the half-space image factor
2, implemented in the GREENAM headers ({src/GREENAM}).  The integral
over each emitting path segment is evaluated by adaptive
Gauss-Legendre quadrature.  Because the integrand carries the source
parameters at the time of emission, sites in the cooling tail
"remember" the widths and power that heated them.  Sites above the
laser plane are vacuum and get T0.

{keyhole} (double ellipsoid).  Two ellipsoidal Gaussian sources share
the laser path:

TOP cap   : typically wide and shallow, centered {z_top} below the
            laser plane (usually 0); carries {f_top * lambda Q}.
BOT depth : typically narrow and deep, centered {z_bot} below the
            laser plane; carries {f_bot * lambda Q} and models the
            keyhole channel. :pre

The vacuum cutoff stays at the real laser plane.  The two lobes are
integrated independently and summed, so a query costs about twice the
{standard} mode.

:line

[Path and multi-pass behaviour:]

The scan comes from "laser_path"_laser_path.html, which must follow
this command.  The line form ({start ... end ... speed V}) supports
{repeats N}, {hatch S N} with optional {serpentine}, a fixed {pause T}
between passes, a temperature-gated {pause_below Tk}, and
{reset_temperature Tr}; the {file} form reads a multi-layer waypoint
path.  Each lobe gets its own GREENAM scan with the shared geometry,
its own center depth ({z_top} or {z_bot} below the laser plane) and
its own power fraction.  The scan starts at the simulation time at which
{laser_path} is issued; issuing {laser_path} again replaces the scan
and discards the thermal history of the previous one.

{pause T} (static schedule): all passes are placed on the timeline up
front, separated by zero-power gaps of {T} seconds; without {pause} the
gap is 1e-12 s (back-to-back).  The integral keeps the decaying tail of
every earlier pass. :ulb,l

{pause_below Tk} (dynamic schedule): only the first pass is scheduled
up front.  Once the current pass has finished emitting and the global
peak temperature has dropped to {Tk} or below, the next pass is
appended at the current time and the scan (and any fluctuation table)
is rebuilt.  The gap therefore follows from the cooling, not from the
input.  After the last pass has finished and cooled below {Tk}, the
run advances straight to its end. :l

{reset_temperature Tr} (with either pause keyword): at each inter-pass
boundary the scan history is cleared, only the new pass is kept, and
the source's ambient T0 is set to {Tr}, so the next pass starts from a
uniform field at {Tr}.  With {pause T} this switches to the dynamic
schedule, firing each pass {T} seconds after the previous one ended. :l,ule

When no site is above the threshold (the liquidus, or {Tk} under
{pause_below}), a fast-forward predictor steps through the remaining
emitting segments in increments of {fast_forward_search_window} (see
"app_style additive/texture"_app_additive_texture.html), evaluating
the integral at the point of the local subdomain closest to the laser,
and skips to the first time it reaches the threshold.

:line

[Stochastic source fluctuations:]

"laser_fluctuations"_laser_fluctuations.html, issued between this
command and {laser_path}, attaches a noise stream that scales each
lobe's lateral widths, depth width and power at every emission time.
In {standard} mode omit the {top|bot|both} selector; in {keyhole} mode
it is required.  Because the integrand uses each emission's own
factors, the cooling tail keeps the fluctuations that produced it.
Total absorbed power is not conserved:

P_total(t) = lambda Q \[f_top (1 + dP_top(t)) + f_bot (1 + dP_bot(t))\] :pre

:line

[GPU support:]

Both modes run in {additive/texture} and {additive/texture/kk}.  In the
Kokkos app every per-site Green's-function integral is evaluated on the
device, for {standard} and {keyhole}, with or without fluctuations, and
for the static and dynamic pass schedules (the host makes the per-pass
firing decisions and rebuilds the scan; the device does every fill).
With {temperature_smooth} enabled the Kokkos app uses the host path.

:line

[Restrictions:]

Q, lambda, k, alpha, cp and every lobe's sx, sy, sz must be > 0.  In
{keyhole} mode z_top, z_bot, f_top and f_bot must be >= 0 and f_top +
f_bot must equal 1 within 1e-6.  Extra trailing arguments are ignored.

The {card} form supports {standard} mode only.
